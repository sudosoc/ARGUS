# Acceptable use

SUDOSOC ARGUS collects information that is already public and puts it together. That is useful for security research,
due diligence, journalism, investigations you are authorised to carry out, and checking your own exposure. It can also be
misused. This page says what the authors intend; it is guidance and a statement of intent, **not a licence condition**
(the licence is the AGPL-3.0, see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

**Use it for:** authorised security work, fraud and abuse investigation, due diligence, research and journalism,
verifying your own or your organisation's exposure, and teaching.

**Do not use it to:** stalk, harass, intimidate or profile private people; find a person's home address in order to
approach or harm them; discriminate against anyone; commit fraud; impersonate anyone; or gather data in breach of the
law that applies to you or of the terms of the services you query.

Things to keep in mind:

* **A result is a lead, not a fact.** A name or a username is not an identity. Verify before you act on or share anything.
* **You are responsible** for having a lawful basis and any needed authorisation, and for following data-protection law
  (for example GDPR) and the terms of every service, wherever you operate.
* **The tool does not log in, solve CAPTCHAs or evade protections.** Sources that need those are listed as manual leads.
* **The data is personal data.** Saved cases and exports contain what was collected; keep them private, keep only what you
  need, delete what you no longer need.
* The authors do not host any data and are not responsible for how the software is used.

If you find the project being used to harm people, you can tell us (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for contact).
