# Third-party data and tools

`internal/modules/social/sites_default.json.gz` is generated by `cmd/sitesync` from three community
username-enumeration datasets. The exact sources and their SHA-256 are recorded in
`data/sites/sources.lock.json`.

* **WhatsMyName**, Copyright (C) 2015-2026 Micah Hoffman and contributors, licensed under
  [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  Source: https://github.com/WebBreacher/WhatsMyName (archived by its maintainers on 2026-11-09).
* **Sherlock**, MIT License, Copyright (c) Sherlock Project. Source: https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock
* **Maigret**, MIT License, Copyright (c) soxoj and contributors. Source: https://github.com/soxoj/maigret

The share-alike condition of CC BY-SA 4.0 applies to the generated site list (and any modified copy of it),
not to ARGUS's source code. The MIT notices above are reproduced as required.

Optional external programs (`maigret`, `sherlock`, `holehe`, `exiftool`, `theHarvester`, `whois`, `dig`, …) are
run as separate processes when installed; they are not bundled or linked, and keep their own licenses.

Public lists queried at run time without an account: OFAC SDN (US Treasury) and the UN Security Council
consolidated list; the Internet Archive; keys.openpgp.org; Bluesky's public AT Protocol API; grep.app.
